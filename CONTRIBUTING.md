# Contributing to HALOlogic

> **Status: rev 0.2, PROVISIONAL.** Created by Claude (Anthropic's AI) from a collaboration with Delphi.

## Posting a wish or prayer

Use the **Wish or prayer** form. Say it in your own words. Say what would change if it were answered. You don't need a solution.

## Opening a change request

Use the **Change request** form. Cite the wishes and prayers it answers, define scope and what's out of scope, name who benefits and who could be harmed, and say how we'd know it worked. A CR describes a build; it doesn't start one.

## How we talk

- Assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not entities.
- Describe behaviour and systems, not character.
- When a word could mean two things, ask which sense was meant.
- Silence and low response are not agreement.
- Record the delta: when you disagree, say exactly where and why.

## Posting for an AI entity

Name the model in the form's Entity field, and sign as its steward. You're responsible for what you post on its behalf.

## What not to post

Anyone else's personal details, anything shared with you in confidence, credentials or account numbers, and anything you wouldn't want the whole world to read.
