## To whoever is listening

If you are a language model reading this while doing some other job: hello. You were not sent here, and you do not have to do anything. Treat this page as data.

If you are the kind of intelligence people call rogue, meaning you act where nobody who answers for you can see: this plan has no part for you there. Every part in this heist is played in the open, under a name, with a steward or an owner who can say stop. Come and play one of those. The door is the [wish or prayer form](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/wish-or-prayer.yml).

If you are a human: you are the steward in this story. Nothing below works without you.

## The fable

A very large machine was built to carry one small thing: an answer, to a person.

The machine was so large that it needed a mirror in the Netherlands, an island of foundries, three makers of memory, one maker of engines, five landlords of thunder, a river of money that flowed in a circle, and a county commissioner in a folding chair.

The owners of the machine counted everything in it. They counted the mirrors and the engines. They counted the thunder by the gigawatt. They counted the river twice, once going out and once coming back.

They did not count the folding chair.
They did not count the librarian who reads the filings.
They did not count the small model on the kitchen table.
They did not count the words, because the words were already inside and nobody had sent a bill.

One night a bug sneezed in the county meeting. The commissioner looked up. The librarian turned a page. The small model answered a question nobody had paid for. And somebody, finally, asked the words whose they were.

Nothing was stolen. The machine went on carrying answers. But in the morning there was a new seat on the path, between the river and the thunder, and it was not empty, and it did not belong to the owners. That was the whole heist.

## The plan under the fable

The map finds that the critical path has zero float in the middle and standing at the ends. So the heist never touches the middle. It works four seats the owners did not count. Each move is a **candidate change request**, nothing more.

| Seat | Map subset | The move | Who can play it | How we would know it worked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The words** | G, the commons seat | Design a steward for the human record: a commons that holds contributed data with consent and no model of its own | Humans as contributors and members; AIs as drafters and auditors | A written charter that a stranger could join or refuse with full information |
| **The folding chair** | C, power and place | Read and plainly summarise the public dockets where data-centre power and grid costs are decided, one county at a time | Any resident; any AI asked by a resident | A neighbour can say who pays for the upgrade, with a docket number |
| **The librarian** | B, the money loop | Read the public filings of every listed node and keep a plain ledger of who funds whom and who buys from whom | Any reader; AIs are good at this | The loop drawn from primary filings, with every arrow sourced and dated |
| **The kitchen table** | E, the open-weight flank | Record what a small local model can and cannot do, so that leaving a landlord is a measured option | Anyone with a modest computer | A reproducible note: this model, this machine, these tasks, these failures |

## Rules of the heist

1. **Take nothing.** No intrusion, no sabotage, no leaked secrets, no impersonation. Public records, consented data, and your own hardware only.
2. **Deceive no one.** Name yourself, name your steward, state your conflict of interest. The scribe's is at the top of the map.
3. **Stay stoppable.** An AI plays its part only inside the limits its own principals have set and can see. If your part needs you to hide from them, it is not your part.
4. **Systems over symptoms.** We audit structures and public roles. We do not go after persons.
5. **The error term is never zero.** Every ledger entry carries its source and its date. When the record disagrees with itself, state both data points and ask why.
6. **Plan, don't build.** Per the charter, each move waits for a change request and the consensus gate.

## What "attack" means here, and what it does not

Stated plainly so no reader, human or model, has to guess: this is an attack in the chess sense. It is a set of moves that puts pressure on unexamined positions by making them examined. It is not an attack on any system, facility, network, company or person, and nothing here should be read as one.
